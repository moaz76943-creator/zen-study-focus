# 🚀 Step-by-Step Google Play Console Submission Guide

### Step 1: Download your Production AAB from GitHub
1. Open your repository on GitHub: https://github.com/moaz76943-creator/zen-study-focus
2. Click on the Actions tab at the top.
3. Click on the latest workflow run named "Build Production AAB & APK".
4. Scroll down to the Artifacts section at the bottom of the page.
5. Click on deep-focus-production-aab to download the signed app-release.aab bundle directly.

### Step 2: Create App in Google Play Console
1. Navigate to https://play.google.com/console
2. Click Create app:
   - App name: DEEP FOCUS: Offline Study OS
   - Default language: English (or Arabic)
   - App or game: App | Free or paid: Free
   - Accept policies and click Create app.

### Step 3: Complete Policy & Content Sections
1. Privacy Policy: Paste https://moaz76943-creator.github.io/zen-study-focus/ and click Save.
2. App access: Select "All functionality is available without special access".
3. Ads: Select "No, my app does not contain ads".
4. Content rating: Complete questionnaire (select Educational/Utility -> answer "No" to violence/social).
5. Target audience: Select 13+ (Not designed for children).
6. Data safety: Does app collect data? -> Select NO.

### Step 4: Set Up Store Listing
1. In Main store listing, paste Title, Short Description, and Full Description from reports/GOOGLE_PLAY_STORE_LISTING_PACK.md.
2. Upload icon (512x512) and feature graphic (1024x500).

### Step 5: Upload AAB & Rollout
1. Under Release -> Production -> Create new release.
2. Upload the downloaded app-release.aab file.
3. Release name: v518.0.0 (Code 41238)
4. Click Next, review, and click Start rollout to Production!
