## Required production acceptance

1. Current Commons original page with author, exact license, dated rights evidence, pinned SHA-1 and SHA-256 and **bit-for-bit** downloaded originals.
2. Full `ffprobe` Vorbis check and lossless-to-null `ffmpeg` decode, duration limit and byte limit; distinguish originals from final loop derivatives with independent hashes.
3. Human sound-by-sound screening against music, speech, bells, artificial noises, unwanted harsh/peak bursts or false titles. Reject/mute offending sounds. Do not trim or sanitize an original silently.
4. Independent 30+ minute loop and crossfade seam listening through Android phone speakers, headphones, Bluetooth, locked screen, audio focus interruptions, DND and background timer.
5. Record explicit per-file QA acceptance and rights provenance; update the **existing** app mixer / `nature_library_v2` only after acceptance and Track 07-09 coordination. Never auto-enable on successful CI alone.
6. Maintain 20 locale names with human-reviewed context and Western digits. Avoid assuming translated labels prove actual sound content.
7. QA artifact sources must stay offline, file-based and in isolated audio listening build; zero runtime network downloads; no music or synthesizer fallbacks; no changes to v21 or normal app.
