# Privacy Policy for DEEP FOCUS

**Effective Date:** November 11, 2026

**Package Identifier:** `com.moaz76943.zenfocus`  
**Developer Contact:** `moaz76943-creator` (GitHub Repository)  

---

### 1. Sovereign Commitment to Zero Data Collection
DEEP FOCUS is fundamentally engineered as a **100% Sovereign, Completely Offline** study operating environment. We believe that focus requires absolute mental sovereignty and unimpeachable privacy. 

- **No Personal Identifiable Information (PII):** We do not collect, request, record, or transmit names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or identifiers.
- **Zero Network Permissions:** The application manifest contains zero network or internet permissions (`android.permission.INTERNET` is strictly prohibited). The application physically cannot communicate with external servers.
- **No Third-Party SDKs or Trackers:** Zero advertising networks, zero analytics frameworks (e.g., Firebase, Google Analytics), and zero crash-reporting services are embedded in the application.

---

### 2. Local Device Storage
All user configurations, custom study intervals, local sound volume preferences, and study session counters reside exclusively within your device's isolated local sandboxed storage (`localStorage` / sandbox databases). 
- Your study statistics never leave your device.
- Uninstalling the application or clearing application cache completely purges all stored preferences.

---

### 3. Audio & Sensor Architecture
DEEP FOCUS generates soundscapes (e.g., binaural beats, pink noise, brown noise) entirely through real-time mathematical procedural digital signal processing (Web Audio API) directly on the local device.
- No audio is recorded, streamed, or uploaded.
- Microphones, cameras, and biometric sensors are neither requested nor utilized.

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy Protection
Because DEEP FOCUS does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information of any kind from any user, it fully complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K, and global child safety standards.
